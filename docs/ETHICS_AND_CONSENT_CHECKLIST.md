# Research Ethics and Consent Checklist

Before onboarding any participant or clinical image data, the following institutional compliance gates must be verified and signed off by the principal investigator:

- [ ] **IRB / REC Approval**: Institutional Review Board / Research Ethics Committee formal approval protocol number documented.
- [ ] **Participant Information Sheet (PIS)**: Provided to participant in their preferred language explaining the research objectives, non-diagnostic prototype nature, and data storage terms.
- [ ] **Signed Informed Consent Form (ICF)**: Verified and stored physically or in an institutional digital consent ledger.
- [ ] **Anonymization & Pseudonymization Check**:
  - Image free of patient face, hospital badges, identity bracelets, and identifiable background markers.
  - EXIF metadata completely scrubbed.
  - Pseudonym assigned in format `PART-XXX`.
- [ ] **Data Transfer Agreement (DTA)**: Executed if importing multi-site hospital datasets.
- [ ] **No Commercial Resale Clause**: Reconfirmed that research data will not be shared with unapproved commercial third parties.
