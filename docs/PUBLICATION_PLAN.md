# Academic Publication Strategy & Venue Analysis

This document provides a realistic, non-guaranteed roadmap for peer-reviewed academic dissemination of the DFU-XAI research findings.

---

## Target Journals & Conferences

1. **IEEE Journal of Biomedical and Health Informatics (J-BHI)**
   - **Scope**: Machine learning in medical imaging, longitudinal clinical modeling, mobile health.
   - **Verification**: Indexed in SCIE / Scopus, Q1.

2. **Computer Methods and Programs in Biomedicine (CMPB - Elsevier)**
   - **Scope**: Medical software prototypes, algorithmic validation, clinical decision support tools.
   - **Verification**: Indexed in SCIE / Scopus, Q1.

3. **Medical Image Computing and Computer Assisted Intervention (MICCAI) Workshops**
   - **Scope**: Diabetic Foot Ulcer Challenge (DFUC), domain adaptation, self-supervised medical imaging.

---

## Submission Integrity Checklist
- [ ] No claim of autonomous diagnosis or clinical validation without full prospective clinical trial.
- [ ] Complete disclosure of dataset source licenses and ethical approvals.
- [ ] Pre-registration of experiment hypotheses and statistical power analysis.
- [ ] Release of open-source de-identified replication package.
